Our Story

Built by a Veteran. Run Like One.

Poor Boy Rides started with a simple idea: Harley owners shouldn't have to wait weeks or pay dealership prices for routine service. After serving our country, our owner turned a lifelong love of riding into a shop built on the same values: show up, do the job right, and treat people square.

We keep it simple. No upsells, no runaround. Just straight answers and quality work on the bikes we love.
